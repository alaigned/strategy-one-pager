# Verdana Foods — Where next? (internal memo)

> Synthetic test fixture — Verdana Foods is a fictional family-owned Czech food producer.
> Deliberately messy: this case tests extraction from an immature strategy document.

From: Tomáš (owner)
To: leadership circle
January 2026

Colleagues,

after the family council in December I promised to write down where I think Verdana should
go. This is not a strategy document, just my thinking — poke holes in it.

We are at roughly €45M revenue, 380 people, two plants (Brno, Vysočina), still profitable but
margins keep sliding. My father always said we make honest food our grandparents would
recognize, and I want that to stay true whatever we do next.

What I believe: we should aim to roughly double the business in five years. I don't see that
happening in the Czech market alone — domestic retail is squeezing everyone. Export today is
about 8% of revenue, mostly Slovakia. Germany, Austria and Poland are the obvious step. Jana
had first conversations with two German chains at Anuga; there is genuine interest in our
smoked line, but they will demand IFS certification, which Brno doesn't have.

Second thing — the plants. Lines 2 and 4 are from the nineties; maintenance ate €1.1M last
year and we lose capacity every summer. If we're serious about doubling volume we need to
replace them, probably a phased program through 2028. We have reserved about €8M CAPEX for
this, which should also count as one of our strategic initiatives in my view.

Third — "Digitalization". Everyone says it, so I'll say it too. Concretely what annoys me:
orders still arrive by email and get retyped by three people, we run the company on
spreadsheets and a 2009 ERP, and I cannot tell you our margin per SKU without two weeks of
Excel work. Marek also keeps pushing his e-shop idea (direct-to-consumer). Maybe. I'm not
convinced it's the priority, but I don't want to kill it either.

There is also the eternal question we keep postponing: own brand versus private label. Today
we're 60/40. Private label pays the bills but the margin is thin and the German conversations
are actually about private label first. I think this decision is mine and the family's to
make, and we should make it by summer, not outsource it to a consultant.

On people — our people are our strength, everyone stays for years, and I want an
entrepreneurial mindset in the next generation of leaders. Whether that's a "strategic
initiative" I honestly don't know.

Organizationally nothing changes for now: Petr runs Production, Jana runs Sales (and would
run export). That's how we'd split any of this work.

Tomáš
