# Perpetua Logistics — where we're going (note from the founder)

> Synthetic test fixture — Perpetua Logistics is a fictional Dutch cold-chain

> single short founder's note.

From: Sanne de Vries (founder & CEO)
To: the management team
March 2026

Two pages, no consultants.

Perpetua hauls temperature-controlled freight — pharma and fresh food — between the Benelux,
Germany and France. Six depots, 240 owned trailers, €120M revenue last year. We exist so that a
pallet of vaccines or berries arrives exactly as it left; that is the whole company in one
sentence.

Where I want us by 2030: €200M revenue, pharma at half of our volume instead of today's 15%, and
temperature excursions under 0.1% of shipments.

Three things get us there.

**Certified cold chain.** Pharma work needs GDP certification on every depot, not only
Rotterdam. And we cannot sell a verified cold chain while 80 of our trailers still report
temperature on paper — telemetry on every trailer, live.

**Contracted instead of spot.** Two-thirds of revenue is still spot business booked by the week.
I want the 30 largest accounts on multi-year lane contracts, priced on the certified service we
will finally be able to prove. Two GDP tenders are already open on Joost's desk — those are the
anchor pharma shippers we should win.

**One record.** None of this works while shipment data lives in three systems and the temperature
trace lives in the trailer. One backbone, one verified record per shipment.

That is it. Petra runs Operations (depots, fleet, planning), Joost runs Commercial (accounts,
pricing, tenders). Bring me a plan per area.

Sanne
