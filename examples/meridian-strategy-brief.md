# Meridian Logistics — Strategy Brief 2026–2028

> Synthetic test fixture — Meridian Logistics is a fictional mid-size
> Central-European parcel & freight company. Any resemblance to real
> companies is coincidental.

## Who we are

Meridian Logistics moves parcels and palletized freight for e-commerce and
industrial customers across Central Europe (AT/CZ/PL/SK), operating three
sortation hubs (Vienna, Brno, Katowice) and a fleet of ~900 vehicles. We
exist to keep commerce moving for the communities we serve.

## Where we want to be

By 2030 we want to be the most reliable logistics partner in Central Europe —
the network businesses build on without thinking twice. For this strategy
horizon (end of 2028) that means: 99.2% on-time delivery, the #1 NPS among
CEE logistics providers, and 30% of revenue from services launched after 2025.

## How we win

### Battlefield 1: Network Excellence

Best-in-region delivery reliability at competitive unit cost.

- **Hub automation rollout** — all three hubs run automated sortation by end
  of 2027, cutting cost per parcel by 25%. Runs January 2026 – December 2027.
- **Dynamic routing platform** — live route optimization across the full
  fleet reduces average delivery time by 15%. Runs June 2026 – June 2027.

### Battlefield 2: Customer Platform Growth

Customers manage everything digitally and stay because it's effortless.

- **Self-service customer portal** — 80% of customer interactions handled
  through the portal without human touch.
- **SME acquisition engine** — 5,000 active SME accounts by end of 2027 with
  customer acquisition cost below €120.

## What makes it possible

Two company-wide enablers, funded centrally:

- **Unified data platform** — one source of operational and commercial truth.
- **Frontline talent program** — hire, train and retain the drivers and hub
  operators the automated network still depends on.

## How we behave

Reliability before speed. Straight talk. Ownership at the edge.

## Organization

Execution is owned by two units: **Operations** (network, hubs, fleet) and
**Commercial** (sales, customer platform, marketing). Network initiatives
belong to Operations; customer platform initiatives belong to Commercial.
